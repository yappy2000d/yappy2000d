### 👤 About Me

I'm an Electronic Engineering student (Computer Science track) who loves hanging out in different tech communities and using programming to build efficient, fun, and automated solutions.

Before getting into hardware design, I spent a lot of time on software development—mostly staying active in the Minecraft modding and open-source localization communities. Between 2019 and 2023, curiosity led me to explore AI and Machine Learning. Later on, I dived into embedded systems and low-level C/C++ programming, which naturally sparked my interest in **Hardware/Software Co-design**.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Digital Circuit Design and RTL.
- 🌱 **Previously explored:** AI/Machine Learning, Embedded Systems, and Open-source localization.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Verilog/SystemVerilog, Python scripting, HW/SW Co-design, or Minecraft modding.
- 📝 **Blog:** I write tech notes and share my thoughts in Mandarin at [blog.yappy2000.com](https://blog.yappy2000.com).
- 📫 **Let's connect:** Always happy to chat, collaborate, and make new friends! Let's learn, build, and break things together! 🚀
